# Cars & Taxis

Car and taxi reservations are part of the Plan timeline but are excluded from the [Flights](FLIGHTS.md) tab. When present, they appear in the dedicated **Cars & Taxis** tab with their route, dates, location, status, and notes.

## Requirements

The TREK public share must enable **Bookings**. If Bookings are not shared, Guest Portal cannot expose transport reservations including Cars & Taxis.
