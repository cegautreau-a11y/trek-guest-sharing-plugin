# Third-party services and upstream projects

TREK Guest Portal integrates with or references:

- **TREK** — self-hosted travel planner; Guest Portal is an unofficial third-party project.
- **Mapbox GL JS / Mapbox services** — interactive Plan map.
- **Immich** — optional original photo capture-date lookup.
- **AeroDataBox** — optional live flight schedule/status provider.
- **adsb.fi** — optional live aircraft data.
- **TREK Flight Tracker plugin** — optional source for a one-time AeroDataBox-key migration helper; not required by the hardened runtime.

Use of these services is subject to their own licenses, terms, quotas, and privacy policies.
